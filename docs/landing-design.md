# Landing page refinement

Mobbin references inspected for hierarchy and image placement:

- [Klarna: Find better deals](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/81368750-7daf-4615-8a68-2006b9361221): one headline, a clear action, surrounding product imagery.
- [Klarna: shopping hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b3833d9a-eddc-4e65-93fd-39045dcd837d): open space around the primary task.
- [Loom Shop](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/095d014e-3d91-4684-b137-1c26ac79b120): large image tiles and restrained supporting copy.

Payday retains its own palette, typography and local concept images. No reference assets were copied. Motion is our own implementation, not inferred from the reference screenshots.

Removed repeated eyebrows, hero captions, the principles strip, long example descriptions, and the second memory example. Privacy and purchase limitations remain in the FAQ.

Finite image entrances, one-time section reveals, staggered steps, preview transitions and hover feedback add movement without autoplay or an endless loop. Content remains visible without IntersectionObserver; reduced-motion preferences disable animation and movement. Preview selection stays explicit and announces its updated content through a polite live region.
