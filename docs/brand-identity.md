# Payday identity

The rounded lowercase p and orange discovery spark represent a passing interest becoming a thoughtful choice.

- Symbol: `public/brand/payday-mark.png` (original image-generated transparent artwork).
- Wordmark: lowercase **payday**, Manrope 750 on the web, deep forest green.
- Core colours: forest `#263C30`, warm ivory `#F8F7F2`, burnt orange `#C2643D`.
- Keep the symbol clear and use an ivory backing on dark surfaces.
- The navigation uses the symbol with live text for accessibility. The pitch uses the same symbol and an editable wordmark.
- Do not use the logo as evidence of retailer affiliation or product endorsement.
